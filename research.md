# Research

<section class="research-detail" aria-labelledby="quantum-rf">
  <h2 id="quantum-rf">Quantum Sensing (Rydberg Atomic Receivers)</h2>
  <p>I study how the response of a Rydberg atomic ensemble can be used to recover a radio-frequency signal. My work connects the atomic response to receiver bandwidth, noise, and sensitivity, and examines how the vector response of a vapor cell can support polarization and direction-of-arrival estimation.</p>
  <p>A current focus is wideband RF-to-optical transduction through six-wave mixing, including how the auxiliary fields affect the receiver’s frequency response.</p>

<section class="research-detail" aria-labelledby="near-field-communications">
  <h2 id="near-field-communications">6G Near-Field Communications</h2>
  <p>Wavefront curvature becomes significant as antenna apertures grow. I study how near- and far-field propagation can be represented in a common wavenumber domain, and how that representation informs channel models and transmission design across link distances.</p>
  <ul class="research-papers">
    <li><a href="{{ '/publications/#unifying-far-near-field' | relative_url }}">Unifying Far-Field and Near-Field Wireless Communications in 6G MIMO</a></li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="research-detail" aria-labelledby="near-field-mimo">
  <h2 id="near-field-mimo">XL-MIMO / Holographic MIMO</h2>
  <p>For electrically large and densely sampled apertures, I study how to use wavenumber, angle, and distance information to recover the channel. One focus is how to model structure in the channel and exploit it to reduce estimation overhead.</p>

<section class="research-detail" aria-labelledby="reconfigurable-intelligent-surfaces">
  <h2 id="reconfigurable-intelligent-surfaces">Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS)</h2>
