title: Quantum Sensing (Rydberg Atomic Receivers)
overview: 'I study how the response of a Rydberg atomic ensemble can be used to recover a radio-frequency signal.
  My work connects the atomic response to receiver bandwidth, noise, and sensitivity, and examines how the
  vector response of a vapor cell can support polarization and direction-of-arrival estimation.


  A current focus is wideband RF-to-optical transduction through six-wave mixing, including how the auxiliary
  fields affect the receiver’s frequency response.'
